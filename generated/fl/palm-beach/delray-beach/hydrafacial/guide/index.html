What to know about hydrafacial

A HydraFacial is a non-invasive hydradermabrasion treatment that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts and hydrates the skin with serums in one visit; it is usually priced per session, with tiered versions (signature, deluxe, platinum) and booster add-ons raising the price. There is no downtime. Ask which tier and boosters a provider recommends for your skin.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a HydraFacial cost in Delray Beach?

Published Florida med spa prices for a HydraFacial most commonly run $150–$350 per session. Signature (basic) sessions are typically $150–$200, deluxe versions with boosters $200–$300, and platinum tiers $275–$350. Packages and memberships usually reduce the per-session cost.

How often should you get a HydraFacial?

Every four to six weeks is the standard recommendation, matching the skin's roughly 28-day cell-turnover cycle. Acne-prone or congested skin sometimes starts with sessions every two to three weeks before stretching to monthly maintenance. Going more often than every two weeks is generally discouraged, because the built-in exfoliation can over-stress the skin barrier.

What does a HydraFacial treat?

It is best for surface-level concerns: dullness, dehydration, congested pores and blackheads, uneven tone and mild texture. The treatment is gentle enough for all skin types, including sensitive skin. For deeper concerns such as pitted acne scars or skin laxity, collagen-building treatments like microneedling tend to deliver better results.

Is there any downtime after a HydraFacial?

No — that is one of its main draws. Most people leave with an immediate glow and return to their day right away; some notice mild redness for a few hours. Makeup can usually be applied the same day, and daily sun protection helps preserve the result.
