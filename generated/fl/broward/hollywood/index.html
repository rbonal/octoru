Frequently asked questions

How much does a med spa treatment cost in Hollywood?

It varies by treatment — injectables are priced per unit or per syringe, while laser, skin and body treatments are usually priced per session or as a package. Octoru shows each Hollywood provider's real starting price where they publish one, and a request-a-quote option where they don't, so you can compare before booking a consultation.

Where can I find laser and body treatments in Hollywood?

Several Hollywood providers list laser hair removal and laser or body-contouring services alongside injectables and skin treatments. Each Octoru listing shows the treatments a provider offers and its verified Google rating, so you can compare the ones near you before booking.

How do I choose a med spa in Hollywood?

Compare verified Google ratings and review counts, confirm which treatments each provider offers, and check the injector or clinician's credentials at your consultation. Hollywood sits in south Broward with providers spread from downtown toward the beach and the Hallandale Beach line, so it is worth comparing a few on rating, price and location first.

Are Octoru's ratings and prices accurate?

Ratings are sourced through permitted Google APIs, not scraped review text, and prices reflect what providers publish. Listings reflect publicly available information, and providers can claim and correct their own listing.
