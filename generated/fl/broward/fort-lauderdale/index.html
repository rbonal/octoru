Frequently asked questions

How much do aesthetic treatments cost in Fort Lauderdale?

It varies by treatment — injectables are priced per unit or per syringe, while laser, skin and body treatments are usually priced per session or as a package. Fort Lauderdale has Broward's deepest provider market, so prices are competitive. Octoru shows each provider's real starting price where they publish one, and a request-a-quote option where they don't.

How do I choose a med spa in Fort Lauderdale?

Compare verified Google ratings and review counts, confirm which treatments each provider offers, and check the injector or clinician's credentials at your consultation. With many providers within a short drive, it is easy to compare a few on rating, price and location first.

Which areas of Fort Lauderdale have med spas?

Providers are spread across downtown, Las Olas and the beach corridors, with more inland toward the western suburbs. Octoru lists Fort Lauderdale providers with their address and verified Google rating so you can find one near you.

Are Octoru's ratings and prices accurate?

Ratings are sourced through permitted Google APIs, not scraped review text, and prices reflect what providers publish. Listings reflect publicly available information, and providers can claim and correct their own listing.
