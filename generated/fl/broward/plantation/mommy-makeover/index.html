About mommy makeover

A mommy makeover combines procedures — commonly a tummy tuck, breast surgery, and liposuction — into one surgical plan addressing changes after pregnancy. Because it bundles multiple surgeries, it should be performed by a board-certified plastic surgeon in an accredited facility, with recovery planned accordingly. Pricing is customized to the combination chosen; request a personalized quote and staged recovery guidance at your consultation.

General guidance only — not medical advice. Confirm specifics with a licensed provider during your consultation.

What procedures are included in a mommy makeover?

It is a customizable combination, most often a tummy tuck plus breast augmentation or lift, frequently with liposuction. The plan is tailored to the changes you want to address after pregnancy. A board-certified plastic surgeon will help you decide which procedures to combine safely in one operation.

What is mommy makeover recovery like?

Because it bundles multiple procedures, recovery is more involved than any single surgery — typically two to three weeks before returning to light work and about six weeks before vigorous activity. Planning help with childcare and household tasks is strongly advised. Your surgeon will provide a staged recovery plan.
