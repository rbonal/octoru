About liposuction

Liposuction removes localized fat through small cannulas to contour areas such as the abdomen, flanks, thighs, or chin. It is a surgical contouring procedure, not a weight-loss method, and works best for stubborn fat in people near their goal weight. Have it performed by a board-certified plastic surgeon in an accredited facility. Pricing depends on the number and size of areas; ask for a quote and recovery plan at your consultation.

General guidance only — not medical advice. Confirm specifics with a licensed provider during your consultation.

These are referential market figures, not quotes: each clinic sets its own rate, so ask for a current price before you book.

Is liposuction a weight-loss procedure?

No. Liposuction contours specific areas of stubborn fat — it is not a treatment for obesity or a substitute for diet and exercise. The best candidates are near their goal weight with localized fat that resists lifestyle changes.

What is liposuction recovery like?

Most people return to desk work within a few days to a week and wear a compression garment for several weeks to manage swelling. Bruising and firmness are normal early on, and the final contour emerges over one to three months. Your surgeon will give you an activity timeline.
