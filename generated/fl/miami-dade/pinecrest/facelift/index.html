About facelift

A facelift (rhytidectomy) tightens and repositions facial and neck tissues to soften deep folds and jowls. It is a surgical procedure under anesthesia, and results look most natural with an experienced board-certified surgeon. Techniques range from mini to deep-plane lifts. Pricing reflects a surgical fee plus anesthesia and facility costs. Ask which technique suits your anatomy and what recovery to expect at your consultation.

General guidance only — not medical advice. Confirm specifics with a licensed provider during your consultation.

Frequently asked questions about facelift in Pinecrest

How much does a facelift cost in Pinecrest?

A facelift in South Florida commonly runs $8,000–$18,000+ depending on the technique (mini vs. deep-plane), the surgeon, and anesthesia and facility fees. Request a quote at a consultation with a board-certified surgeon.

How long does a facelift last?

A facelift turns the clock back rather than stopping it; results commonly last roughly 8 to 12 years, though aging continues naturally afterward. Skin quality, technique, and lifestyle all affect longevity. Many patients maintain results with non-surgical treatments over time.

What is the difference between a mini and a deep-plane facelift?

A mini facelift addresses early jowling with shorter incisions and a quicker recovery, while a deep-plane facelift repositions deeper tissue layers for more comprehensive, longer-lasting rejuvenation. The right choice depends on your anatomy and goals — a board-certified surgeon will recommend the appropriate technique at your consultation.
