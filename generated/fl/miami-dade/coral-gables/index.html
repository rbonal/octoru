Frequently asked questions

How much does a med spa treatment cost in Coral Gables?

It depends on the treatment. Injectables such as Botox are priced per unit and fillers per syringe, while laser, skin and body treatments are usually priced per session or as a package. Octoru shows each Coral Gables provider's real starting price where they publish one, and a request-a-quote option where they don't, so you can compare before booking a consultation.

How do I choose a med spa in Coral Gables?

Compare verified Google ratings and review counts, check which treatments each provider actually offers, and confirm the injector or clinician's credentials at your consultation. Coral Gables has several long-established aesthetic practices, so it is worth comparing a few on rating, price and location before you book.

Do Coral Gables med spas offer consultations in Spanish?

Many do. Coral Gables and the wider Miami-Dade area have a large bilingual community, and a number of listed providers offer consultations in both English and Spanish. Each listing notes the languages a provider lists.

Are the ratings on Octoru real?

Yes. Ratings are sourced through permitted Google APIs — Octoru does not scrape or republish review text. Listings reflect publicly available information, and providers can claim and correct their own listing.
