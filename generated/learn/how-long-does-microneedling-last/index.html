How long does microneedling last?

A single microneedling session gives a fresh, glowing look that often lasts several weeks, while the collagen-building benefits keep developing for months. Most providers recommend a series of sessions for longer-lasting results.

What to expect after one session

In the days to weeks after microneedling, skin can look brighter and smoother as it heals. This initial glow commonly lasts around 4 to 6 weeks. Underneath, the treatment stimulates collagen, which keeps improving skin texture over the following months.

How long the results last

Because microneedling works by building collagen, results are cumulative. A course of about 3 to 6 sessions spaced a few weeks apart tends to produce the most noticeable, longer-lasting improvement — often holding for several months. Many people maintain results with a session every 6 to 12 months.

What affects longevity

Your skin concern, skincare routine, sun protection, and whether you complete a full series all affect how long results last. Consistent sun protection helps preserve the improvement.

Frequently asked questions

Is one microneedling session enough?

One session gives a temporary glow, but most providers recommend a series of 3 to 6 for lasting collagen-building results.

How often should you get microneedling?

A common approach is an initial series a few weeks apart, then maintenance every 6 to 12 months depending on your goals.
