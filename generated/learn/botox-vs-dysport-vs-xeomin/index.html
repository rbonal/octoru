Botox vs Dysport vs Xeomin

Botox, Dysport and Xeomin are all botulinum-toxin type-A injectables that soften dynamic wrinkles. They all work; they differ in dosing, spread, and onset speed, not in whether they are effective.

The key differences

Dysport units are not 1:1 with Botox units and it tends to spread a little more, useful for larger areas like the forehead. Xeomin is a purified formulation. Onset is roughly 2 to 4 days for Dysport and 3 to 7 days for Botox and Xeomin; all last about 3 to 4 months.

Cost comparison

Because unit counts differ between products, per-unit price is misleading. Always compare the total quoted for your treatment, not the sticker per-unit rate.

How to choose

Pick the injector first and the product second. An experienced provider recommends a product based on your treatment area and history.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dysport cheaper than Botox?

Sometimes per unit, but Dysport typically uses more units, so compare total cost.

Which one lasts the longest?

All last roughly 3 to 4 months; duration depends more on dose and metabolism than brand.
