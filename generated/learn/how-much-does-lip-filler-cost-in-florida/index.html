How much does lip filler cost in Florida?

Lip filler is priced per syringe, so your total depends on how many syringes you need and which product your injector uses. In Florida, a single syringe of hyaluronic-acid lip filler commonly runs from the mid-hundreds to around a thousand dollars, with Miami-area medical spas often at the higher end. Your exact price comes from a consultation.

How lip filler pricing works

Most lip fillers are hyaluronic-acid (HA) products such as Juvederm or Restylane, sold by the syringe (often 0.5 mL or 1 mL). A first-time treatment frequently uses half a syringe to one full syringe. Because pricing is per syringe, two people can pay very different totals depending on how much product they need.

What changes your price

Injector experience and credentials, the brand and amount of filler, your treatment goals, and any current promotions. Metro markets like Miami tend to price higher than smaller Florida towns. For a medical injectable, the lowest quote is not automatically the best choice — who injects you matters.

How to get an accurate quote

Most Florida providers give you a firm price at a free consultation after seeing your lips and goals. Compare verified providers near you using the links below.

Frequently asked questions

Is lip filler priced per syringe or per session?

Almost always per syringe. Ask how many syringes your provider recommends and multiply by the per-syringe rate.

How much is a full syringe of lip filler in Miami?

Miami-area pricing for one HA syringe commonly falls in the mid-hundreds to around a thousand dollars; compare several verified providers for an exact quote.
