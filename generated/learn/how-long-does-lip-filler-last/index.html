How long does lip filler last?

Most hyaluronic-acid lip fillers last about 6 to 18 months, with many people seeing results hold for roughly 9 to 12 months before a touch-up. How long yours lasts depends on the product, your metabolism, and how much was placed.

Typical timeline

After the initial swelling settles (usually one to two weeks), your final result appears. HA lip filler then gradually breaks down over months. Smaller amounts and faster metabolisms tend to fade sooner; larger placements can last longer.

What affects how long it lasts

The filler brand and formulation, how much product was used, your metabolism and activity level, and how the area moves all influence longevity. Lips are highly mobile, so lip filler often fades a bit faster than filler in less-active areas.

Maintaining your results

Many people schedule a touch-up once or twice a year. HA fillers can also be dissolved with hyaluronidase if you want to reverse or adjust them. A qualified injector can recommend a maintenance schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Does lip filler last longer the more you get it?

Some people find results hold a little longer with repeat treatments, but this varies. Longevity depends mostly on product, dose, and metabolism.

Can lip filler be dissolved early?

Yes. HA lip filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase if you want to reverse it.
