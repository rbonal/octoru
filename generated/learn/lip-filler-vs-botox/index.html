Lip filler vs Botox

Lip filler and Botox are different tools for different jobs. Lip filler adds volume and shape using hyaluronic acid; Botox is a muscle-relaxing injectable that softens wrinkles caused by movement. Many people confuse them, but they treat different concerns.

What each one does

Lip filler (an HA product like Juvederm or Restylane) plumps and defines the lips by adding volume. Botox (a botulinum-toxin injectable) relaxes specific muscles to smooth dynamic lines such as frown lines and crow's-feet. A 'lip flip' is a small Botox technique that gently everts the upper lip — it is not the same as adding filler volume.

How long they last

HA lip filler typically lasts about 6 to 18 months. Botox usually lasts about 3 to 4 months. Because Botox wears off faster, it needs more frequent touch-ups.

Cost and choosing

Filler is priced per syringe; Botox is priced per unit — so the two are not directly comparable on price. Pick your goal first: more volume and shape means filler; softening movement wrinkles means Botox. An experienced injector can combine both when it makes sense.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get lip filler and Botox at the same time?

Yes, providers often combine them, for example filler for lip volume plus a Botox lip flip. Discuss your goals at a consultation.

Which is cheaper, Botox or lip filler?

They are priced differently (units vs syringes) and treat different things, so compare total treatment cost, not sticker rates.
