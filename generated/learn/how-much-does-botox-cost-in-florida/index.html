How much does Botox cost in Florida?

Botox is priced per unit, so your total depends on how many units your treatment areas need. In Florida, per-unit pricing typically runs in the low-to-mid teens, with Miami-area medical spas often at the higher end. The only way to get your exact number is a consultation.

How Botox pricing works

Clinics charge per unit. A frown-line treatment commonly uses about 20 units; forehead lines and crow's-feet add more, so two people can pay very different totals at the same per-unit price. Ask how many units a provider recommends and who performs the injection.

What changes your price

Injector experience, the number of areas treated, the product used (Botox, Dysport or Xeomin), and any current promotions. For a medical injectable, the cheapest quote is not automatically the best choice.

How to get an accurate quote

Most Florida providers quote at a free consultation. Compare verified providers near you using the links below.

Frequently asked questions

Is Botox priced per unit or per area?

Almost always per unit. Compare the total units times the rate.

Why is Botox more expensive in Miami?

Higher-demand metro markets and experienced injectors often price at the higher end. Compare several verified providers rather than assuming.
