How long does Botox last?

Botox typically lasts about 3 to 4 months. First-time patients sometimes notice it wears off a little sooner, and results can vary with the dose, the area treated, and your metabolism.

Typical timeline

Botox usually starts working within 3 to 7 days and reaches full effect around two weeks. The muscle-relaxing result then gradually fades over roughly 3 to 4 months as the product wears off and movement returns.

What affects how long it lasts

The number of units used, the specific muscle treated, your metabolism, and how expressive the area is all matter. Under-dosing to save money often means results fade faster, so the cheapest treatment is not always the best value.

Keeping results consistent

Many people book touch-ups every 3 to 4 months to maintain a smooth look. Staying on a regular schedule can help results look consistent over time. Your injector can recommend a cadence based on how you respond.

Frequently asked questions

Does Botox last longer over time?

Some regular patients find they need slightly less product or fewer sessions over time, but the typical duration stays around 3 to 4 months.

Why did my Botox wear off quickly?

Lower doses, a very active muscle, or a faster metabolism can shorten how long it lasts. Discuss dosing with your injector.
